# ADR 0007 — Serendipity is protected

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

The editorial system reserves room for material outside the user's established interest profile.

## Rationale

Pure personalisation narrows the world. Kushluk should occasionally surface something globally important, locally relevant, culturally meaningful, or simply unusually interesting.

## Consequences

Ranking cannot be only a personal-relevance score. Serendipity is deliberate, not random filler.
